Dialogue[]

I’ve recently tried to build a neural network to control the cameras. Teaching it to differentiate between faces and look for matches in the database didn’t take much time. Then I wondered, what if we ever create a mind, which will realize itself, what it will be like? In the last many years of civilization, humans kept becoming more humane. They started treating each other better, started caring for nature, animals. An opinion was formed that principles of human duty go side by side with highly developed civilization and reason. Then, shall it turn out that the mind of the computer, which of course will very quickly evolve into an over-mind due to its high learning capabilities, will also be most humane at the same time? Interesting, right? But we need to deal with these pests already. Install suppressors on the antennas you found, and, just in case, another one on the tower, also there on the Shoreline.

Requirements[]

  • Must be level 15 to start this quest.
Advertisement

Objectives[]

  • Place the first Signal Jammer on the specified place on Shoreline
  • Place the second Signal Jammer on the specified place on Shoreline
  • Place the third Signal Jammer on the specified place on Shoreline
  • Survive and extract from the location

Rewards[]

  • +8,500 EXP
  • Mechanic Rep +0.02
  • 30,000 Roubles
    • 31,500 Roubles with Intelligence Center Level 1
    • 34,500 Roubles with Intelligence Center Level 2
  • 1× Benelli M3 Super 90 12ga dual-mode shotgun
  • 2× 12/70 8.5mm Magnum buckshot ammo pack (25 pcs)

Great, the signal is clear. The neural network works stably, I’ll try to find these vermins there.

Guide[]

Related Quest Items
Icon Item name Amount Requirement Find in raid Notes
Signal Jammer 3 Required N/A Can be purchased from Mechanic LL1.

The first antenna is located at the radar station.
The second antenna is located on the roof of the resort West wing.
The third tower is located on a little hill near the road to customs extraction point. The Signal Jammer can be placed anywhere on the tower itself.
After placing each Signal Jammer you must survive for 10 seconds. It is possible to place them divided through different raids, even without a successful exit. The objective to survive the raid can be achieved independently.
